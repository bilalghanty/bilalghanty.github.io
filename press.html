01 / BIOGRAPHY

Short bio

Bilal Ghanty is an IB Diploma student at Le Bocage International School in Mauritius with hands-on experience across business, technology, operations, engineering-related projects and motorsport.

Extended background

Outside school, he is actively involved at Neetoo Industries across procurement, sourcing, administration, technology, manufacturing-related projects, business operations and special projects. He is also involved in developing Quads Mauritius and works on projects involving AI, digital experiences and practical technology.

He also contributes to short-term rental operations, including guest communication, bookings, property coordination and day-to-day operations.
