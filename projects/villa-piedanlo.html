02 / From browsing to a conversation

Choose dates.
Ask the host.

The direct-booking route is a request to stay. Final availability and the rate are confirmed personally by the host.

  1. 01

    Dates & guests

    Choose the stay dates and number of guests. The website checks for clashes with its availability calendar.

  2. 02

    A prepared enquiry

    The details open in WhatsApp, ready for the guest to send to the host.

  3. 03

    Personal confirmation

    The host replies with availability and the rate. No payment is taken on the website.
