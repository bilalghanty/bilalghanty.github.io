02 / THE WORK, END TO END

From the search
to the sale.

  1. 01

    Vehicle research

    Compare models, specification and condition against what makes sense for Mauritius.

  2. 02

    Supplier sourcing

    Find suppliers, compare quotations and arrange purchases.

  3. 03

    Costing & pricing

    Work out purchase, freight and landed costs, then set the selling price and margin.

  4. 04

    Imports & logistics

    Coordinate shipping, documents and the practical work of getting the quads to Mauritius.

  5. 05

    Sales & customers

    Handle enquiries, explain the vehicles, agree sales and follow through with buyers.

  6. 06

    Day-to-day decisions

    Keep track of the money, follow up on orders and decide what to source next.

03 / RUNNING THE BUSINESS

The decisions.
The sales.
The responsibility.

Quads Mauritius is a profitable business with substantial turnover from local quad sales.

I handle the whole process, including the money committed to vehicles, imports and the costs around each sale.

The supplier’s price is only part of the decision. Freight, landed cost, selling price and money tied up before a sale all affect the margin.

It keeps me involved in both the vehicles and the numbers behind the business.
