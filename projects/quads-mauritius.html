My involvement

I’m involved in the development and operations of Quads Mauritius, working across different parts of the business rather than focusing on only one area.

This gives me hands-on experience with the practical side of an automotive and motorsport business — from researching vehicles and suppliers to understanding logistics, costs and the decisions involved in bringing products to the Mauritian market.

OPERATIONS / 01—06

Behind the vehicles.

  1. Vehicle Sourcing

    Researching quad bikes, specifications, suppliers and suitable models for the Mauritian market.

  2. International Suppliers

    Supplier communication, quotation analysis and coordination involved in sourcing vehicles and related products.

  3. Imports & Logistics

    Learning and contributing to shipping, freight, import costs, documentation and the logistics behind bringing vehicles to Mauritius.

  4. Business Operations

    Working on pricing, landed costs, product planning and other operational decisions involved in developing the business.

  5. Automotive Research

    Comparing models, engines, specifications, parts and technical differences across different quad bikes and motorsport vehicles.

  6. Motorsport

    Exploring projects around quad bikes, karting and motorsport, combining technical interest with practical business experience.

Why Quads Mauritius matters to me

Motorsport has been one of my strongest interests for years. Quads Mauritius gives me the opportunity to understand the industry beyond simply enjoying the vehicles themselves.

It lets me combine automotive knowledge with business, logistics, international sourcing and practical problem-solving.

Part of a bigger picture

The experience I gain through Quads Mauritius complements the work I do through Neetoo Industries and my other technology and engineering projects. Together, these projects let me learn through real situations instead of only through theory.
