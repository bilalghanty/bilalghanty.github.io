My involvement

Neetoo Industries is a family-owned manufacturing business in Mauritius, and I’m actively involved in a range of real-world projects across the company. My role is not limited to one department — I often work across technology, administration, sourcing, operations and business projects depending on what needs to be done.

I’m regularly trusted with responsibilities that involve working alongside senior management and contributing to projects that affect different parts of the business.

AREAS OF PRACTICE / 01—06

Across the business.

  1. Technology

    Digital systems, office technology, access-control systems, digital tools and technology projects.

  2. Administration

    Supporting company processes, documentation, internal coordination and administrative work.

  3. Sourcing & Suppliers

    Supplier research, RFQs, quotations, component sourcing and communication with manufacturers and suppliers.

  4. Manufacturing Projects

    Work involving product components, tooling, materials, production-related sourcing and manufacturing requirements.

  5. Business Operations

    Pricing, logistics, imports, supplier coordination and operational projects.

  6. Special Projects

    Projects that do not fit into one department, including digital experiences, process improvements and new ideas for the company.

Why this matters to me

Working inside a real manufacturing business gives me the chance to learn by solving actual problems. It lets me combine business, technology, engineering and practical decision-making instead of keeping those interests separate.

Selected project

One of the projects I completed was a 360° virtual showroom for Neetoo Industries, using high-resolution panoramic photography to create an interactive online experience.
