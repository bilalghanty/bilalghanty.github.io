Mauritius Telecom
AI Video Contest

A creative AI project where I explored how artificial intelligence can be used to develop original digital media and visual storytelling for the Mauritius Telecom AI Video Contest.

The Project

I took part in the Mauritius Telecom AI Video Contest as an opportunity to experiment with AI-powered creative tools and turn an idea into a complete digital video project.

The project combined artificial intelligence with creativity, visual storytelling and digital production.

What I Worked On

Concept Development

Developing the idea and direction behind the video.

AI Tools

Exploring artificial intelligence tools for generating and developing creative media.

Visual Storytelling

Turning generated content into a clear and engaging visual story.

Digital Production

Bringing the different creative elements together into a finished project.

What I Learned

The project helped me better understand both the possibilities and limitations of AI-generated media. It also gave me practical experience using AI as a creative tool rather than only learning about the technology in theory.

Why It Fits My Interests

Artificial intelligence is one of the areas of technology I want to keep exploring. Projects like this let me combine computer science, creativity and experimentation while learning how new tools can be applied to real projects.
