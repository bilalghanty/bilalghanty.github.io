03 / People in the record

Bill.
The longer view.

Belal “Bill” Ghanty is my father. His involvement in Mauritian karting extends across technical work and organisation.

Contemporary reporting in 2004 places him in a technical role on a championship committee. In 2005, coverage describes race direction, organisation and scrutineering.

A 2022 Défi Moteurs feature returns to the practical work: maintaining and repairing karts, supporting drivers and organising regular sessions.

A separate competition story

Farhan
Ghanty.

My uncle Farhan is Bill’s younger brother. Contemporary coverage names him as the 2005 champion in the 135 category, a weight category described in the period’s reporting.

In December 2006 he competed in National 100 at the Trophée de l’Océan Indien in Réunion. He finished fourth on track, before disqualification at the technical checks.

04 / Tecnikart today

A machine.
A working operation.

Tecnikart Racing is the family karting business, technical operation and racing activity I’m involved in today.

The machines still need preparation, parts and attention. The photographs alongside them hold a longer record of the sport.

05 / My part in it

Alongside the work.
Increasingly hands-on.

I work alongside Bill on kart preparation, parts, tyres and troubleshooting, and help organise track days and races.

01 Preparation / parts / tyres
I help check what needs attention, prepare tyres and parts, and work through mechanical issues with Bill to get karts ready for track days and races. Bill is the more experienced technical lead; I increasingly handle parts of the preparation myself.
02 Planning / logistics / admin
I help coordinate with drivers and kart owners, prepare equipment, work through logistics and handle parts of the organisation and administration around the day.
03 On the day
Preparing equipment and helping with the practical work around track days and races.
