01 / PROJECT NOTES

The Project

The aim of the project was to create a more modern way for people to experience the Neetoo Industries showroom digitally. Instead of relying only on conventional photographs, the showroom was captured in 360° so visitors could explore the space in a much more interactive way.

02 / PROJECT NOTES

My Role

I worked on the creation and development of the virtual showroom, including capturing the showroom through high-resolution 360° photography and developing the digital experience around it.

This project is part of my wider involvement at Neetoo Industries, where I contribute to technology, digital systems, administration, business operations and special projects.

03 / PROJECT NOTES

Why I Built It

I wanted to explore how technology could improve the way a traditional manufacturing business presents its products and physical spaces online. The project combines photography, digital experience design and practical business use.

04 / PROJECT NOTES

What I Learned

The project gave me hands-on experience with 360° imaging, digital presentation and turning a technical idea into something that can actually be used by a business.
